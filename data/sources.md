# Sources for the launch dates

Dates were gathered on 2026-09-28 from the comparison at
<https://rywalker.com/research/personal-agents-platforms> (45 products), then each product's first
public launch was looked up separately, and eight more agents launched in the last month were added
(FO, FAMBOT, CATCH, OPENMUSE, SAI, RENE, HAVEN, RABBIT OS3 - category "Newest launch").

The column `what_the_date_marks` in [`agents.csv`](agents.csv) says what each date represents; it is
not always a formal launch (repo creation, first commit, Show HN, press announcement, ...).
`~` dates are **estimates** (VIOLA from its terms-of-service date, LAZZY from its privacy-policy date).
Open-source projects that were renamed use the date of the first public code under the original name.

Checked against the original announcement or vendor page:
Haven (PR Newswire, 2026-09-17), rabbit OS3 (rabbit newsroom, 2026-09-22), Sai (Simular, 2026-09-16),
Fambot (TechCrunch, 2026-09-01), Catch (Unite.AI, 2026-09-02), Grok Bot (x.ai, 2026-08-11).
Fo and Rene could not be confirmed from the vendor's own pages; OpenMuse's date comes from a Show HN post
(two other projects share the name).
